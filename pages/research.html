Research Themes

Our research explores the geometry of data and models across multiple areas.

Graph Neural Networks

Understanding how GNNs generalize to unseen nodes, through optimal transport and geometric perspectives, and improving node classification.

  • Transductive Generalization via Optimal Transport – NeurIPS 2026

  • Posterior Label Smoothing for Node Classification – AAAI 2026 (Oral)

  • Bridging Generalization and Expressivity – ICLR 2025

  • Reverse Process GNN for Heterophilic Graphs – ICML 2024

Trustworthy AI

Developing methods for machine unlearning, privacy-preserving ML, and robust evaluation of AI systems.

  • Holistic Unlearning Benchmark – ICCV 2025

  • Few-shot Unlearning – S&P 2024

  • Robust Evaluation of Diffusion Purification – ICCV 2023 (Oral)

ML for Science

Applying machine learning to scientific discovery, including drug design, molecular modeling, and computational biology.

  • High-order Equivariant Flow Matching – NeurIPS 2025 (Spotlight)

  • Multi-Reward Drug Design – ICML 2025

Language Models

Understanding and building reliable LLM agents: how harness design interacts with post-training, multi-turn collaboration with experts, energy-efficient multi-agent systems, and multimodal reasoning.

  • Long Live the Librarian! A Persistent Search Sub-Agent – EMNLP 2026 (Oral)

  • In-Place Feedback for Expert-LLM Collaboration – EMNLP 2026 (Oral)

  • Harness Design and Post-Training in LLM Agents – Findings of EMNLP 2026

  • Plane Geometry Problem Solving: A Survey – Findings of EACL 2026

  • CoPL: Collaborative Preference Learning – EMNLP 2025

Generative Models

Understanding the learning dynamics of diffusion models, composing pre-trained generators for multi-objective generation, and generating structured data such as graphs.

  • Learning Dynamics Across Loss and Prediction Choices for Diffusion Models – NeurIPS 2026

  • Routing by Reaching: Composing Pre-trained GFlowNets – ICML 2026

  • Hierarchical Graph Generation with K2-trees – ICLR 2024

  • Feature Unlearning for GANs and VAEs – AAAI 2024
